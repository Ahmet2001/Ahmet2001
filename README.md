## ⚡ Currently building

> **Language-model systems that are smaller, faster, measurable, and actually useful.**

- Native inference runtimes and portable model formats
- Alternative LM architectures, routing, and tokenization
- KV-cache efficiency, quantization, CUDA/Triton kernels
- Turkish SLMs and evidence-grounded reasoning models
- Tool-using agents and local-first AI products

<summary><h2>🧠 Research & Language Models</h2></summary>

<br/>

| Project | Focus |
|---|---|
| [**RelationLM**](https://github.com/Ahmet2001/RelationLM) | Experimental language-model architecture built around explicit anchor–partner relations, sparse routing, RelationLex tokenization, and custom Triton selection kernels |
| [**ForesightLM**](https://github.com/Ahmet2001/ForesightLM) | Sentence-level future-signal learning, calibration, semantic reranking, and self-loop hard negatives in small language models |

| [**RiskKV**](https://github.com/Ahmet2001/RiskKV) | Risk-aware heterogeneous KV-cache compression for preserving exact-critical context under aggressive compression |
| [**QA-Research-SLM**](https://github.com/Ahmet2001/QA-research-SLM) | Evidence-grounded research reasoning, claim verification, structured outputs, and verifier-guided small-model training |
